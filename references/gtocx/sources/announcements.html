GTOC X Winners :: NUDT&XSCC

Congratulations to the joint team of the National University of Defense Technology and the State Key Laboratory of Astronautic Dynamics, Xi'an Satellite Control Center for securing the best solution for GTOC X by the close of the intense competition! Well-done to the Team Leads, Ya-Zhong Luo and Hong-Xin Shen, along with their team members Yue-He Zhu, An-Yi Huang, Zhen Yang, Zhen-Jiang Sun, Bing Yan, Jin Zhang (NUDT), and Tian-Jiao Zhang, Zhao Li, Jian-Hui Li, Zheng-Zhong Kuai, Jun Liang (XSCC).

A great thank you to all the teams who dedicated their non-trivial efforts to make this competition a very exciting one to follow.

Submission Limit going up

Starting at 6AM UTC, 23 May, teams will be able to submit 24 solutions per sliding 12 hour window. Successful trial submissions still count towards the limit.

The Submission Period has now started (8pm UTC 21 May). Congratulations to Team Kataskopoi for putting the first solution on the Leaderbaord within the first minutes!
